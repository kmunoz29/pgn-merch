FAQ

How do I pay?

Two ways: Cheddar Up or cash. When you place your order, the confirmation screen gives you a Cheddar Up link — pay there and put your Order # in the payment note so we can match it up. If you'd rather pay cash, hand it to any exec at chapter and mention your Order #. Either way we'll text you to confirm.

Where's my order? / I lost my confirmation number

Enter the email address you ordered with and we'll send your confirmation number and order summary straight to your inbox.

Didn't give an email when you ordered? Text us and we'll look it up in our records.

Will my order list still be there if I close the page?

Yes. Items you've added stay in your order list on that device, so you can come back later and finish. Once you actually place the order the list clears itself. Note this is per-device — a list started on your phone won't show up on your laptop.

How do I get my order?

Two ways: pick it up at chapter, or have it delivered. If you'd rather grab it at chapter, leave the address blank at checkout and mention pickup in the notes.

When will my order be ready?

Once your payment is confirmed, we'll coordinate a pickup or delivery time and text you when it's ready.

What size should I get?

Most tees run true to size. If you like a relaxed or oversized fit, size up. Text us if you're unsure.

Have an idea for new merch?

Yes! Use the "Got an idea?" button up top to submit your idea — we take chapter suggestions for future drops.
